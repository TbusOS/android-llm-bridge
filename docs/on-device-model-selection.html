Picking the on-device model for a commercial Android product, worldwide.全球商用场景下，设备端 Agent 的模型怎么选。

Design note · 2026-04-21 · owner: sky · status: recommendation设计笔记 · 2026-04-21 · 作者：sky · 状态：recommendation

Companion to the on-device agent design note. That page answers why and how the host talks to the device. This page answers which model, and what hardware it needs — for products shipped globally, with a 5–8 year lifetime, and legal teams that read licenses carefully.这是设备端 Agent 设计笔记的配套文档。那一页讲「为什么做」和「host ↔ device 协议」；本文讲「设备端那个本地模型具体选哪个、需要什么硬件」—— 面向全球销售、5–8 年生命周期、法务会仔细看许可的硬件产品。

  3. Model backend registry模型后端注册表openai-compat takes a custom addressopenai-compat 可指定地址
TL;DR一句话结论

Phi-3.5-mini (MIT) + llama.cpp, on ARMv8.2 devices with ≥ 4 GB RAM.Phi-3.5-mini（MIT）+ llama.cpp，跑在 ARMv8.2、≥ 4 GB RAM 的设备上。

  • Default: Microsoft Phi-3.5-mini Q4_K_M (~2.4 GB). MIT license, strong English reasoning, tool calling works.默认：Microsoft Phi-3.5-mini Q4_K_M（约 2.4 GB）。MIT 许可、英文推理强、tool calling 可用。
  • RAM-constrained (≤ 3 GB): IBM Granite 3.2-2B (Apache 2.0). Explicit patent grant, enterprise-friendly.RAM 受限（≤ 3 GB）：IBM Granite 3.2-2B（Apache 2.0）。显式专利授权，企业法务好过。
  • Hard floor: ARMv8.2-A dotprod + 3 GB RAM + arm64 Android 10+. Below that, don't ship on-device agent — route everything to host.硬门槛：ARMv8.2-A dotprod + 3 GB RAM + arm64 Android 10+。不达标就不要上设备端 agent，全部走宿主端。
  • Avoid for 5–8 year global SKUs: Gemma (unilateral policy changes), Llama (700M MAU clause), Mistral non-commercial, Apple OpenELM (distribution restricted).5–8 年全球 SKU 慎用：Gemma（条款可单方变动）、Llama（700M MAU 条款）、Mistral 非商用、Apple OpenELM（分发受限）。
Constraints约束

Four constraints that rule everything else out.四条约束，把大多数候选过滤掉。

Global + commercial + hardware is stricter than most LLM demos ever face. These four, in order, decide what is even on the table.全球 + 商用 + 硬件，比大多数 LLM demo 场景严得多。下面四条依次决定了什么东西能上桌。

1

Licensing legal can greenlight at a glance.许可要干净到法务一眼放行。

No “700M MAU and we renegotiate” (Llama), no unilaterally-updatable Prohibited Use Policy (Gemma), no “Built with X” branding obligation. Once a device ships, you cannot chase changed terms back.不能有「700M MAU 要另谈」（Llama），不能有事后可单方改的 Prohibited Use Policy（Gemma），不能有「Built with X」品牌标注义务。硬件一旦出厂，条款变了你也追不回。

2

Hardware ceilings vary wildly.硬件天花板差异极大。

Entry-level devices have 2–4 GB RAM, mid 4–6, high 8+. SoCs span Snapdragon 8 to Allwinner A53 — many orders of magnitude apart. Industrial and kiosk builds usually have no GMS or AICore, which kicks Google's on-device stack out of contention.入门 2–4 GB RAM，中端 4–6，高端 8+。SoC 从骁龙 8 系到 Allwinner A53 跨几个数量级。工控 / 车机 / IoT 多数没有 GMS 或 AICore —— Google 的设备端方案直接出局。

3

What the device actually has to do.设备真正要干的事。

English is enough (host translates). Structured output + tool calling is the core job. Chinese and other languages are the host's problem, not the device's.英文够用（宿主翻译）。核心任务是结构化输出 + tool calling。中文和其他语言是宿主的问题，不是设备的问题。

4

Product lifetime, not demo lifetime.产品生命周期不是 demo 生命周期。

Hardware lives 5–8 years in the field. Weights and licenses have to last that long. A model that depends on a vendor portal still being up in 2032 is already a risk.硬件在现场活 5–8 年，权重和许可也要撑这么久。依赖某个厂商门户 2032 年还活着的模型，就是一个风险。

MIT vs Apache 2.0 for hardware vendors硬件厂商视角：MIT 与 Apache 2.0

MIT is the most minimal permissive license — no explicit patent grant, only an implicit one. Apache 2.0 does grant patents explicitly, and revokes if you sue someone over those patents. For hardware shipped worldwide and exposed to patent trolls, Apache 2.0's patent clause is the friendlier posture. MIT is still enough (Microsoft ships Phi under MIT deliberately) and every corporate legal team knows it.MIT 是最简洁的宽松许可 —— 没有显式专利授权，只有默示。Apache 2.0 显式授予专利，且有专利反制条款（你告我专利，授权自动失效）。对「全球出货、容易被专利钓鱼」的硬件，Apache 2.0 的专利条款更友好。MIT 也完全够用（微软主动选了 MIT 发 Phi），每家企业法务都熟。

Product planning产品规划

Plan three SKUs. Don't try to pick one model for everything.按三条 SKU 规划。不要一个模型打天下。

Hardware lines span tiers. Map each tier to the model it can actually run, write the requirements into the BOM, and fall back to host-only on the lowest end.硬件产品线跨多个档位。把每档映射到它真能跑的模型，要求写进 BOM，最低档退到「纯宿主端」即可。

Field check现场验收

Five-second field check.五秒现场验收。

Given an engineering sample, four commands decide whether on-device agent is viable:拿到一台工程样机，四条命令决定能不能上设备端 agent：

adb shell cat /proc/cpuinfo | grep -E "asimddp|fphp|i8mm|sve"
adb shell cat /proc/meminfo | grep MemTotal
adb shell getprop ro.build.version.release    # need ≥ 10
adb shell getprop ro.product.cpu.abi          # need arm64-v8a
  • asimddp (= dotprod) — present means INT8 inference is on the table. Absent → tier C.asimddp（= dotprod）—— 有：能上 INT8 推理。没有：直接 C 档。
  • fphp + asimdhp — FP16 available, another 1.5–2× speedup.fphp + asimdhp —— FP16 可用，再快 1.5–2×。
  • i8mm — top-shelf (Cortex-A715 / X3+). Rare in shipping products today.i8mm —— 顶配（Cortex-A715 / X3+）。目前量产设备极少见。
  • sve / sve2 — ARMv9. Effectively absent from Android devices right now.sve / sve2 —— ARMv9。当下安卓设备基本没有。
  • MemTotal ≥ 3.5 GB → tier B starting point. ≥ 7 GB → tier A.MemTotal ≥ 3.5 GB → B 档起步。≥ 7 GB → A 档。
Bottom line结论
For a global commercial SKU with a 5–8 year lifetime: Phi-3.5-mini (MIT) + llama.cpp, on ARMv8.2 devices with ≥ 4 GB RAM. Squeeze down to Granite 3.2-2B (Apache 2.0) when RAM is tight. Below ARMv8.2 dotprod, don't bother — route to host.全球商用、5–8 年生命周期：Phi-3.5-mini（MIT）+ llama.cpp，跑在 ARMv8.2、≥ 4 GB RAM 的设备上。RAM 紧时退到 Granite 3.2-2B（Apache 2.0）。ARMv8.2 dotprod 以下 —— 别折腾，全部走宿主端。

Chinese and other languages are handled by the host-side LLM (Qwen2.5-3B, Claude, GPT). The device only speaks structured English. Clean division of labor, no language-training tax on the device fleet.中文和其他语言由宿主端大模型（Qwen2.5-3B / Claude / GPT）承担。设备只说结构化英文。分工干净，设备端零语言学习税。
